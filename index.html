The math under the hood
Each pixel integrates the exact photon orbit equation of the Schwarzschild metric (Binet form, u = 1/r):
d²u/dφ² + u = (3/2) rₛ u²
solved as r̈ = −(3/2) rₛh² r/|r|⁵ with RK4.

Units: rₛ = 2GM/c² = 1. Photon sphere at 1.5 rₛ, disk inner edge at the ISCO 3 rₛ (= 6GM/c²).

Disk: Shakura–Sunyaev profile T ∝ r−3/4(1−√(r₮ₙ/r))1/4, Keplerian Ω = √(GM/r³), local speed v = √(M/(r−2M)). Observed shift g = √(1−rₛ/r) / γ(1−β·n̂); since Iₕ/ν³ is invariant, rendering a Planck spectrum at Tₒₐₙ = g T gives the correct g⁴ beaming automatically. Colors come from Planck's law sampled at R/G/B wavelengths.
