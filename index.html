The math under the hood
Each pixel integrates the exact photon orbit equation of the Schwarzschild metric (Binet form, u = 1/r):
d²u/dφ² + u = (3/2) rₛ u²
solved as r̈ = −(3/2) rₛh² r/|r|⁵ with RK4.

Units: rₛ = 2GM/c² = 1. Photon sphere at 1.5 rₛ, disk inner edge at the ISCO 3 rₛ (= 6GM/c²).

The test object is a sphere hit-tested against every geodesic step. Stars use Planck blackbody at Tᵒᵇᵎ = g T with limb darkening. Planets use Lambertian shading. All redshift & lensing effects are exact — the object gets lensed, Einstein-ringed, and multi-imaged exactly like anything else.
