The math under the hood
Each pixel integrates the exact photon orbit equation of the Schwarzschild metric (Binet form, u = 1/r):
d²u/dφ² + u = (3/2) rₛ u²
solved as r̈ = −(3/2) rₛh² r/|r|⁵ with RK4.

The yellow dashed ring shows the object’s geometric (flat-space) position. The rendered object is its true lensed position — where it appears after following bent null geodesics. The gap between ring and sphere is the gravitational deflection. Toggle “Gravitational lensing” to remove the bending and watch the object jump to the ring.
